Rudrika by Tara, developer build (single zip for chat delivery)

This zip has everything needed to run, review and host the store, except two heavy media
sets that could not travel through the chat channel (30 MB cap):

1. Product photos (224 files, 45 MB). Regenerate them in about a minute, before seeding:
       cd rudrika-web && node scripts/fetch-rudrika-images.js
   They download from the live rudrika.in catalogue into public/uploads/products/.
   The same files are also in Rudrika-final-build-part2 to part6 zips and in the complete
   zip on Dan's Mac (~/Downloads/Rudrika-final-build-2026-09-13.zip).

2. The three campaign films for the design site (21 MB, review only, not needed for hosting).
   They are in Rudrika-final-build-part7 to part9 zips; drop them into
   landing-concepts/rudrika-site/media/ to play them on the design site.

Then follow docs/HANDOFF.md (run steps, admin login, what is built, Razorpay and WhatsApp
switch-on, VPS checklist). Quick start is in rudrika-web/README.md.
